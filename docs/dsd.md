> [!NOTE]
> Per the agreed visual design system specification, **[`docs/design.md`](design.md)** is the canonical source of truth for all styling, typography, colors, layout, and component rules.
> 
> - **Visual Metaphor:** *"The Notebook, Kept"* — Ruled school-notebook paper, ballpen blue ink, red margin line, and yellow highlighter.
> - **Zero Monospace:** Replaced `Geist` / `Geist Mono` with `Atkinson Hyperlegible Next` (400/600/700, `tabular-nums` for amounts) + `Bricolage Grotesque` (700 display) + `Caveat` (500 for `.notebook` handwriting blocks only).
> - **Palette:** `paper` (`#F2F5EC`), `sheet` (`#FFFFFF`), `rule` (`#D5DEC6`), `pencil` (`#23262B`), `muted` (`#5B6457`), `ink` (`#1E3FAE`), `margin` (`#C8302F`), `marker` (`#FFE14D`), `paid` (`#1F7A4D`).
> 
> Refer directly to **[`docs/design.md`](design.md)** for the full token schemas, component contracts, and Do's & Don'ts.
